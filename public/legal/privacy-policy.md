**Device Information:**
- Device type and model
- Operating system and version
- Browser type and version
- Device identifiers (e.g., advertising ID)
- Screen resolution and device settings

**Usage Information:**
- Call logs (date, time, duration, participants)
- Features you use and how you interact with the Service
- Pages or screens you view

- Links you click
- Search queries

**Technical Information:**
- IP address
- Log data (access times, pages viewed, errors)
- Cookies and similar tracking technologies

### 1.3 Information from Third Parties

**Google OAuth:**
- If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address, name, profile picture, and date of birth (if you grant permission)

**Payment Processors:**
- If you make purchases, our payment processors may share transaction information with us (but not your full payment card details)

## 4. Data Storage and Security

### 4.1 Where We Store Your Data
Your information is stored on secure servers provided by Firebase (Google Cloud Platform), which may be located in the United States or other countries.

### 4.2 How We Protect Your Data
We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your information, including:

- **Encryption:** Data is encrypted in transit using TLS/SSL and at rest using industry-standard encryption
- **Access controls:** Limited access to personal information on a need-to-know basis
- **Authentication:** Secure authentication mechanisms (phone verification, OAuth)
- **Monitoring:** Regular monitoring for security vulnerabilities and threats
- **Firestore Security Rules:** Database access is restricted based on user authentication and authorization

### 4.3 Data Retention
We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. You may request deletion of your account at any time.

**Retention Periods:**
- Account information: Until you delete your account
- Call logs: 90 days
- Messages: Until deleted by you
- Analytics data: Aggregated and anonymized after 24 months
- Legal compliance data: As required by law

### 4.4 Security Limitations
While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.
